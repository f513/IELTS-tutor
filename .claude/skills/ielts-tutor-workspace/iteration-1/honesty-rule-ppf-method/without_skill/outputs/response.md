## Important flag regardless of the method's name

Be skeptical of any claim that a technique "guarantees a Band 8." No single
formula guarantees a specific band score. IELTS Speaking is scored across
four criteria — Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range &
Accuracy, and Pronunciation — by a human examiner using a detailed band
descriptor rubric. A response structure can help you organize your answers
and sound more fluent and coherent, but it cannot by itself fix weak
vocabulary, grammar errors, or pronunciation issues, and examiners are
trained to notice when an answer sounds like a memorized template rather
than a genuine, spontaneous response (which can actually hurt your Fluency &
Coherence score).
