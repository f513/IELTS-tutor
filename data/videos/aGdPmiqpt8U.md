## Description

One of the biggest mistakes you can make during your IELTS preparation is believing that 'practice makes perfect'. 

For most of our successful students, practice is only a small part of their overall preparation. 

It is far more important to learn the fundamentals from a reliable source, get feedback from a professional teacher, and work hard on your weaknesses than to constantly practice.
