Harneet needed 8777 on her IELTS test to move to Canada and land her dream job. 

Unfortunately, she kept getting 6.0 or 6.5 on her Writing test. 

With our help, she was able to overcome this obstacle by making a few simple changes. 

In this video, she shares the key changes she made, including: 

- Finding out what the examiner wants and giving it to them; 

- Keeping her language and ideas simple (yes, doing this normally improve your score); 

- Developing her main ideas and reducing the number of ideas; and 

- How she prepared for her test. 

I hope you can learn something from her story and improve yourself. 

Keep it simple!
